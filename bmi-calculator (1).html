Frequently Asked Questions

What is BMI?+
BMI (Body Mass Index) is a value calculated from height and weight, used to screen for weight categories that may lead to health problems.
What is a healthy BMI?+
For adults, a BMI of 18.5–24.9 is considered normal weight. Below 18.5 is underweight, 25–29.9 is overweight, and 30+ is obese.
Is BMI accurate for athletes?+
Not always. BMI may overestimate body fat in athletes with high muscle mass. It is a screening tool, not a diagnostic measure.
