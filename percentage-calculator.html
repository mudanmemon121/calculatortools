Frequently Asked Questions

How to calculate percentage?+
Divide the percentage by 100, multiply by the number. E.g. 15% of 200 = 0.15 × 200 = 30.
How to calculate percentage change?+
% Change = ((New - Old) / Old) × 100. Positive = increase, negative = decrease.
What are percentage points?+
Percentage points are absolute differences between two percentages. Going from 10% to 15% is 5 percentage points, not 50%.
