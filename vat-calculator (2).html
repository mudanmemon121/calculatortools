Frequently Asked Questions

What is VAT?+
VAT (Value Added Tax) is a consumption tax on goods and services, collected at each production stage. The final consumer pays the full VAT.
How to add VAT to a price?+
Multiply the price by (1 + VAT rate/100). E.g. $100 + 20% VAT = $100 × 1.20 = $120.
How to remove VAT from a price?+
Divide the VAT-inclusive price by (1 + VAT rate/100). E.g. $120 ÷ 1.20 = $100 net price.
