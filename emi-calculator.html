Frequently Asked Questions

What is EMI?+
EMI (Equated Monthly Installment) is a fixed monthly payment made to a lender that includes both principal repayment and interest.
How to reduce EMI?+
Increase loan tenure, make a larger down payment, or negotiate a lower interest rate with your lender.
Is EMI the same as a monthly payment?+
Yes. EMI is the Indian banking term for what Western banks call a monthly loan installment.
