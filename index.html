About CalcHub

Built for clarity,
designed for everyone.

CalcHub is a free, professional-grade calculator platform. We believe useful tools should be fast, accurate, beautiful, and accessible to everyone — no paywalls, no signups, no ads. Ever.

Instant results
All calculations happen entirely in your browser. Zero server round-trips, zero latency.
Private by design
We never store, transmit, or log any data you enter. Your numbers stay in your browser.
Precision-first
Every formula is sourced from official references — IRS, ISO standards, WHO BMI guidelines.
No account needed
Open any calculator and start. No email, no password, no payment. Free forever.
Works everywhere
Built mobile-first, fully responsive. Every calculator looks perfect on any screen.
Clean, open code
No frameworks, no dependencies, no build step. Pure, auditable, transparent code.

Our mission

The internet is full of calculator websites cluttered with ads and dark patterns. CalcHub exists to prove it doesn't have to be that way — clean, accurate, fast tools, free for everyone.
